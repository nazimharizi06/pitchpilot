# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last updated: [date]**

This policy describes how billing, cancellation, and refunds work for PitchPilot subscriptions, and is
incorporated into our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).

## 1. Free trial

Eligible new subscribers receive a 7-day free trial on any plan (Base, Pro, or Premium). If you don't cancel
before the trial ends, you will be charged the full price of your selected plan, and your subscription will
begin its normal monthly billing cycle.

To prevent abuse, PitchPilot limits the free trial to one per person, checked by email address and phone
number. If our system detects that your email or phone was already used for a prior trial, your subscription
will be charged immediately, without a further trial period.

## 2. Billing

Subscriptions are billed monthly, in advance, at the price shown for your plan at the time you subscribed.
Prices may change with notice; continuing your subscription after a price change constitutes acceptance of
the new price. `[Lawyer to confirm required notice period for price changes under applicable law.]`

## 3. How to cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings ("Manage subscription"), which opens
Stripe's Billing Portal. Cancellation stops future billing, and **your access continues through the end of
your current paid billing period** — canceling does not immediately cut off access to the plan or drill
library you've already paid for. This matches the cancellation behavior configured in the Stripe Billing
Portal as of this writing; if that configuration ever changes, this section must be updated to match.

**PitchPilot does not offer refunds for partial billing periods, unused time, or "I forgot to cancel"
